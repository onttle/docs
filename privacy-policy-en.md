# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 30, 2026**

PiedPiper ("Company") operates Onttle ("Service") and places great importance on protecting Users' personal information.
The Company complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws, and makes every effort to protect Users' personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how the Company processes Users' personal information in the course of providing the Service.

---

## 1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Onttle Service provided by the Company, together with its related website, API, applications, and other related services.

Onttle provides a service that allows Users to manage content and data and use it through an API. It may also provide AI, search, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), AI Agent, and file storage features.

## 7. Cross-Border Transfer of Personal Information

The Company may use certain overseas service providers to provide the Service.

### 7.1 Supabase

The Company uses Supabase for user authentication and database operation.

The Supabase project used by Onttle uses the following region.

- Region: AWS Seoul
- AWS Region: `ap-northeast-2`

Accordingly, Service data processed through Supabase may be processed and stored in the Seoul, South Korea region.

---

### 7.2 Amazon Web Services

The Company uses Amazon Web Services for file storage and cloud infrastructure.

The AWS region used by Onttle is as follows.

- AWS Region: `ap-northeast-2`
- Location: Seoul, South Korea

Files and Service data uploaded by Users are stored in the Seoul, South Korea region in accordance with the Company's service configuration.

The Company uses OpenAI's API to provide AI features.

A User's data may be transmitted to the OpenAI API to provide the following features.

- OpenAI Chat

- RAG
- AI Agent

In the course of using AI features, prompts, questions, or content and data entered by a User that are necessary for AI processing may be processed through OpenAI's API.

- Prompts
- Questions
- Commands
- Content
- Documents

### 8.2 Use of the OpenAI API

The Company uses the OpenAI API to provide AI features.

To the extent necessary to perform AI features, the following data may be transmitted to the OpenAI API.

- The User's prompts
- Questions and requests
- Content necessary for AI processing
- Data necessary for RAG search
- Text subject to Embedding generation

The Company processes such data only to the extent necessary to provide AI features.

---

### 8.3 Embeddings and RAG

Onttle may analyze content registered by a User and generate Embeddings in order to provide RAG features.

In this process, the following may occur.

1. Content analysis
2. Text extraction
3. Content splitting
4. Embedding generation
5. Storage of vector data
6. Retrieval of related data
7. Generation of AI responses

In the course of generating Embeddings, text or content registered by a User may be transmitted to the OpenAI Embeddings API.

---

### 8.4 AI Agent

Onttle may provide an AI Agent feature.

In response to a User's request, the AI Agent may perform the following functions.

- Generating AI responses
- Searching content
- RAG search
- Retrieving Service data
- Calling the API
- Performing Service features permitted by the User

Where the AI Agent processes Service data, it processes such data only to the extent necessary to carry out the User's request.

---

### 8.5 AI-Generated Output

Output generated through AI features is produced by an automated system.

AI-generated output may not always be accurate, complete, or up to date.

Users should conduct an appropriate review of AI-generated output before using it for important decisions or situations requiring professional judgment, such as legal, medical, or financial matters.
